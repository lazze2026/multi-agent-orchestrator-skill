# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be useful, respectful, and safe for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and trade-offs, not people.
- Keep issues focused and actionable.
- Assume good intent, but respect stated boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information or credentials.
- Deliberately weakening safety boundaries without clear discussion.
- Spam or off-topic promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and issues that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in being blocked from participation.
